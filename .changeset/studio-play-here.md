Add "Play from here" to Studio: right-click the level (or press Ctrl+Shift+Enter with the mouse over it) to play with the player starting at that point. The level's Spawn does not move, checkpoints and restarts go back to the chosen point, a point inside a wall is refused with a message, and replays recorded this way do not load into a normal Play. The Play debugger shows "from here" while such a run is active.
