# Attribution

The pack's art under `assets/` (the Metool and Bat sheets, the Life and Weapon
Energy capsule sheets, and their `enemy_anims.json` / `pickup_anims.json`) comes
from the Mega Man X8 16-bit fan re-creation (the upstream Godot reference
project). The sheets are copied as is; the animation metadata is generated from
its Aseprite sidecars and `.tres` resources by
`apps/studio/scripts/starter-template/build-enemies.mjs` and `build-pickups.mjs`.
